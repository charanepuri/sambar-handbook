🛒 Quick Shopping List (Classic Sambar)

  • Toor Dal – 1 cup
  • Tamarind – Lemon-sized ball
  • Turmeric Powder – ½ to 1 tsp
  • Sambar Powder – 2 tbsp
  • Mustard Seeds – 1 tsp
  • Cumin Seeds – 1 tsp
  • Dry Red Chillies – 2
  • Curry Leaves – 10 to 12 leaves
  • Asafoetida (Hing) – A pinch
  • Onion – 1 medium
  • Mixed Vegetables – 2 cups (Drumstick, Carrot, Pumpkin, Brinjal)
  • Tomato – 1 medium
  • Jaggery – ½ to 1 tsp
  • Salt – To taste
  • Oil or Coconut Oil – 2 tbsp
  • Coriander Leaves – For garnish
