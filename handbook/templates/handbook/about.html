About Sambar Handbook

Sambar Handbook is an educational website dedicated to preserving and celebrating the culinary heritage of South India's most beloved dish, Sambar.

This website provides information about the history, regional varieties, ingredients, recipes, nutritional values, and interesting facts about Sambar.

Our Mission

  • Promote traditional South Indian cuisine
  • Preserve regional Sambar recipes
  • Educate food enthusiasts
  • Encourage healthy cooking practices
