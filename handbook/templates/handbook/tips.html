Sambar Cooking Tips

  • Wash toor dal properly before cooking.
  • Mash dal well for a creamy texture.
  • Add tamarind only after vegetables become soft.
  • Use fresh curry leaves for authentic flavor.
  • Temper with ghee or coconut oil for better aroma.
  • Let the sambar rest for 15–20 minutes before serving.
  • Add a small piece of jaggery to balance tanginess.
